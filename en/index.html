What is Fitosat

What it is
An online service that watches your crops from the sky, whenever you need.
What it does
Spot stressed plants early and treat only where needed.
What you get
Vigour maps, alerts, zone-by-zone risk and a seasonal history.
Who it’s for
Farms, growers’ associations, nurseries, agronomists and advisors.

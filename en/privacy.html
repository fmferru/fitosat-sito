Privacy notice

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This notice explains how Fitosat processes the personal data of people who visit fitosat.it or contact us, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003.

Data controller

Fitosat srl – Via Lanzone 5, 20123 Milan (MI), Italy
Email: info@fitosat.it

What data we process

  • Data you send us. If you write to us through the pilot program form, the “Try it free” button or by email: your name, email address and any information you choose to include (company, crop, hectares, message). The form does not send data to a server: it prepares an email that you send from your own mail app.
  • Browsing data. The service hosting the site automatically logs technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested page, date and time, to keep the site running and secure.
  • Language preference. If you choose a language, the choice is stored only in your browser (local storage) and is never sent to us.

Why we process it and on what basis

  • To reply to your requests and assess participation in the pilot program: pre-contractual steps taken at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To run and secure the site: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not use your data for profiling or advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Cookies and tracking

The site uses no cookies, no visitor analytics and no third-party tracking tools, so no consent banner is needed.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • GitHub, Inc. (Microsoft group), which hosts the site and logs browsing data. Its servers may be located in the United States: transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.
  • Aruba S.p.A., which runs our email inbox in Italy.

How long we keep it

We keep emails and contact details for as long as needed to handle your request and, if no business relationship follows, for no more than 24 months. Browsing data is kept by the hosting provider for the time technically required.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its processing, or receive it in a portable format (Arts. 15–22 GDPR), by writing to info@fitosat.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it).

Changes

We may update this notice; the date at the top shows the latest version.
